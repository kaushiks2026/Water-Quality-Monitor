Multidisciplinary Project (MDP) Scientific & Safety Disclosure

This automated monitoring station measures selected physical and electrochemical parameters (pH, Turbidity, Temperature, Dissolved Oxygen) as an early warning for sudden contamination or environmental shifts. These measurements alone do not prove water potability, as microbial pathogens (e.g. E. coli) and dissolved heavy metals require certified laboratory assays. Documented reference standards are based on BIS 10500:2012 and WHO Drinking Water Guidelines.
